# Pandex

Real law for AI agents and developers: legislation with its past versions and court decisions, collected directly from the institutions that publish them.

- **MCP server and API:** [pandex-mcp](https://github.com/pandex-io/pandex-mcp), at `https://api.pandex.io/mcp`, free to try without a key
- **Docs:** [docs.pandex.io](https://docs.pandex.io) ([source](https://github.com/pandex-io/pandex-docs)) · **Coverage:** [pandex.io/coverage](https://www.pandex.io/coverage) · **Key:** [pandex.io/key](https://www.pandex.io/key)

Held today: 25 countries and the European Union, 24.4 million documents, from the United States, Brazil and Canada to France, Germany and Italy, and the European Court of Human Rights. More countries every week.
