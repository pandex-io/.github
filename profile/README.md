# Pandex

Official European law for AI agents and developers: legislation with its past versions and the decisions of the highest courts, collected directly from the institutions that publish them.

- **Read the law:** [pandex.io/law](https://www.pandex.io/law), article by article, with its versions and the leading decisions that cite it
- **MCP server and API:** [pandex-mcp](https://github.com/pandex-io/pandex-mcp), at `https://api.pandex.io/mcp`, free to try without a key
- **Docs:** [api.pandex.io/docs](https://api.pandex.io/docs) · **Coverage:** [api.pandex.io/coverage](https://api.pandex.io/coverage)

Held: European Union, France, Germany, Netherlands, United Kingdom, Austria, Switzerland, Luxembourg, European Court of Human Rights.
